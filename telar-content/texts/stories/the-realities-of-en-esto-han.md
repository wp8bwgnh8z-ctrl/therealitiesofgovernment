---
title: "\"En esto han de pensar los Americanos, no en pelear unos con otros\""
---

Rodríguez argues that Americans should focus on working together instead of fighting among themselves. Now that Spanish America has the opportunity to build new societies, people should think about what those societies could become and how they should be organized. For Rodríguez, independence is not enough on its own, people must cooperate and actively work toward creating a better society. This shows that his idea of a republic depends on unity, cooperation, and people working together to build their future.
