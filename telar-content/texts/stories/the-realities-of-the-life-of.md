title: "The Life of Simón Rodríguez through the Prism of his Biographers"
---

In the article "The Life of Simón Rodríguez through the Prism of his Biographers," Manuel Peña writes a letter to Simón Bolívar to share information about Simón Rodríguez. Peña highlights Rodríguez's pedagogical model of education and social reform, which aligned with Bolívar's vision for an educated society in Latin America after colonial rule. Peña specifically mentions a project by Rodríguez called "Hospicio," a structure where young people could learn practical skills, such as trades, writing, and math. Rodríguez needed around 2,000 to 3,000 pesos to start this project, and Peña believed it could eventually support itself through the work done there.
