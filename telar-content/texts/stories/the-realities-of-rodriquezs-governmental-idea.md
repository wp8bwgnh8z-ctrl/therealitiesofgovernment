---
title: "Rodriquez's Governmental Idea"
---

The key idea behind Simon Rodriguez's idea on government is to have equilibrium among all citizens. This is learned by initially illustrating Columbus depending on "force" to strip away his citizens rights in order to gain dominance. On the other hand, Rodriguez wants men to be free although to a certain limit which is, laws based on the education of the citizen. This is further developed from the article "The 'Immoral' Educator: Race, Gender and Citizenship in Simón Rodríguez's Programme for Popular Education" by Nicola Miller as it highlights that teaching citizens without cultivating them can be used as a weapon to society. This is significant as this connects to Rodriguez's whole idea of having a free but civilized society that is well educated and taught how to be a good citizen for the nation. Overall, Rodriquez pushes for the sensation of having freedom and equity among all citizens that is based upon the education and responsibilities that every citizen would still have to take upon in order to obtain this freedom in their society.
